# Final video checklist

- [ ] Final MP4 is 60–120 seconds and plays from start to finish.
- [ ] Gazebo and RViz are both visibly genuine, not slides or mockups.
- [ ] Robot, world obstacles, map, scan, paths, and costmaps are readable.
- [ ] A goal is visibly submitted and robot motion is continuous.
- [ ] Inserted obstacle is visible in Gazebo and local costmap.
- [ ] Narration matches actual behavior; any failure is disclosed.
- [ ] No secrets, personal notifications, or unrelated windows appear.
- [ ] Final result and a meaningful conclusion are visible.
